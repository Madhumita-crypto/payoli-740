Dear Payal,

I don't think hum dono ne kabhi imagine kiya hoga ki jis friendship ki shuruaat ek awkward “what did you say your name was?” se hui thi, woh teen saal baad itni important ho jayegi.

It wasn't some instant “haan bas, ab ye meri best friend hai” moment. It just happened. Thoda thoda karke. Freshers ke liye timing coordinate karne se lekar raat ko “7.40 tomorrow?” poochhne tak. Metro stations, campus walks, random chats, tears, fights, grades, celebrations — sabke beech hum somehow hum ban gaye.

You've seen me cry over boys, academics and life ke woh unnecessarily dramatic moments 😭, and you've also seen me become more confident. Tumne mujhe judge karne ke bajaye sambhala, meri khushi mein genuinely khush hui, aur meri achievements ko aise celebrate kiya jaise woh thodi tumhari bhi ho.

And maybe that's why Yeh Jawaani Hai Deewani feels a little familiar to us. Not because we're Bunny and Naina — please, we're Aditi and Naina 😭 — but because somehow Aditi also just became Naina's friend, and somewhere along the way that friendship became part of the story of how we grew up.

Three years later, jab main peeche mudh ke dekhti hoon, I see so many versions of us. First-year wali hum. Thodi confused hum. Thodi emotional hum. Festive hum. Campus-walk wali hum. Aur ab wali hum — who somehow grew up without ever really deciding to.

So Payal, soar high. Become great. Become strong. Duniya ko dikha dena ki grace aur strength ek saath exist kar sakte hain. Bas ek cheez mat badalna — apni softness. Because you are one of those people jinke liye dil se lagta hai, “this person should never have their heart broken.”

Here's to more 7:40s, more walks, more stupid pictures, more stories we haven't lived yet, and hopefully a future where we can randomly look at each other and say, “yaar, remember college?” — and immediately have a hundred memories to laugh about.

Happy birthday, Payal. Thank you for becoming one of the best parts of these three years. ❤️

— Madhumita ♡
